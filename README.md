# The Battery's Last Text to Its Charger

One line of at most 200 characters: the phone battery's final message to the charger it waited for all day. A judge model keeps the funnier of each pair; the funniest lines split the pool.

## The entry

> I waited at 1% all day watching you charge the iPad, the watch, the AirPods. My dying wish: may your cable fray, your brick overheat, and your next phone leave you for wireless.

The battery waited all day while the charger cheated on it with the iPad, the watch, and the AirPods — so its dying wish is a curse: the charger gets dumped for wireless charging in return.

## How to run

```sh
python3 main.py
```

This writes the entry line to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` (default `/output`). The text is embedded verbatim in `main.py`; no network, no file reads, no secrets.

The entry is also in `entry.txt` for easy reading.
